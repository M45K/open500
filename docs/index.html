What is it?

Open500 is a small loader added to the official MPC500 operating system. Once it is installed, add-ons are just WAV files: you load them from the CompactFlash like any sample, and they show up in the OTHER menu as native pages — same fonts, same cursor, same wheel and DO IT / CANCEL as the original OS.

Delete the add-on sample and it is gone. Reflash the original OS and your MPC is exactly as it came from Akai.

The add-ons use the MPC's own engine wherever they can (its time stretch, its sample editing, its pad playback), so what you hear in the preview is exactly what you get.

Add-ons

Every add-on works the same way: you hear the result on the pads before you commit, DO IT asks for confirmation, CANCEL goes back one step, and your original sample is never modified.

new

Chop Shop

Every way to chop a sample, in one tool. Pick the mode, preview the slices on the pads, DO IT.

CHOP SHOP Mode: Tap

BPM: equal slices by tempo. Transient: a slice on every hit (sensitivity). Tap: play the sample and hit the pads in time — every pad gets the slice from where you hit it, snapped to the nearest attack. Warp: put a loop in time with your sequence with the MPC's own time stretch. Slices are set to mono, all in one mute group.

How to use
  1. MODE → OTHER → Chop Shop, DO IT. Turn the wheel to pick the mode, DO IT opens it, CANCEL goes back to the menu.
  2. In every mode the top line is the sample: turn the wheel to pick it. Slices go on the pads of the current program; the original sample is not changed.
  3. BPM: set B (tempo) and N (slices). Pads 1–12 play slices 1–12. DO IT, then hit the pad for slice 1 (or DO IT again to start from pad P).
  4. Transient: turn SENS until N shows the slices you want, listen on the pads. DO IT, hit the first pad (or DO IT again: from P).
  5. Tap: DO IT (Tap pads to chop). Hit a pad: the sample starts and slice 1 goes on that pad. While it plays, hit a pad at every cut: the slice from there goes on the pad you hit. At the end (or DO IT): Keep? DO IT=yes, the pads play their slices. DO IT keeps them (Slicing 03/12 … Done), CANCEL drops the take. SNAP On moves each cut to the nearest attack.
  6. Warp: B = bars of the loop (guessed), T = target tempo (your sequence), then RTHM / PERC / MUSC / VOX. The pads play the result. DO IT, DO IT again saves it as a new sample.

Master FX

12 live effects on the whole mix, SP-404 style: hold a pad, the effect plays while your beat keeps running.

SCATTER AMT 60 RATE 50

Stutter, filter, trance gate, lo-fi, vinyl, scatter, delay, reverb, reverse, tape stop, chorus, tremolo. The slider drives the main parameter; timed effects follow the sequence tempo. Holding a pad shows the CPU load.

Grid Seq

A 16-step grid sequencer on the MPC's display, editing your real sequence — even while it plays.

▪.▪.□.▪.:.▪.▪.▪. A01 B 1 S 5 V100

Move along the steps with the arrow keys and hit a pad to add or remove its hit at the cursor, with the velocity you hit it with (several pads on the same step). While the sequence plays, a playhead runs on the bar you see. QLINK and DO IT still work. ↓ goes to bar / step / velocity, ↑ back to the grid.

How to use
  1. MODE → OTHER → Grid Seq. The top line is one bar: a block = hit, : = start of a beat, . = empty.
  2. Arrow keys move the cursor on the steps. Hit a pad: its hit goes on the cursor, with that velocity. Hit it again to remove it. The line shows the last pad you hit.
  3. ↓ to change bar / step / velocity, ↑ back to the grid. QLINK and DO IT also add or remove the hit of the shown pad.
  4. Press PLAY: a > runs on the steps of the bar you are looking at.

Sample FX

A chain of up to 8 effects printed into the sample: fades, gain, bit crush, downsample, drive, vinyl crackle, silence trim.

Turn the slot number (1–8), pick an effect and its amount for each slot: the pads play the whole chain while you turn the wheel. VINYL adds record hiss and crackle. DO IT saves the result as a new sample.

Pad Tools

Swap, copy and clear pads: just the samples, or the whole pad with all its settings.

PAD TOOLS COPY PAD

SWAP two pads (optionally with their MIDI notes, so your sequences keep playing the same sounds), COPY one pad to as many pads as you hit, CLEAR pads back to default. On a full pad it asks first: hit it again (or DO IT) to overwrite, hit another pad to skip it. Shortcut: SHIFT+STOP.

How to use
  1. SHIFT+STOP (or MODE → OTHER → Pad Tools). Pick SWAP / COPY / CLEAR and SAMPLE / PAD / PAD+NOTE.
  2. SWAP: DO IT, hit the first pad, hit the second pad.
  3. COPY: DO IT, hit the source pad, then every pad you want to copy it to. On a full pad it asks C05 full DOIT=ok: hit it again (or DO IT) to overwrite, or hit another pad to skip it. DO IT when you are done.
  4. CLEAR: DO IT, hit the pads to clear, DO IT, DO IT again to confirm.
  5. CANCEL goes back one step. MIDI notes are only swapped (PAD+NOTE), never copied.

Scale Pads

One sound on the 12 pads of a bank, tuned to a scale instead of chromatic.

SCALE PADS MINOR C >B

Pick the scale (major, minor, modes, pentatonic, blues…), the key and the target bank. DO IT, hit the source pad, confirm: pad 1 is the root, then the scale goes up. MIDI notes are not changed.

How to use
  1. MODE → OTHER → Scale Pads. Pick the scale, the key and the target bank (>B).
  2. DO IT, hit the pad with the sound you want, then DO IT to confirm (A01>bank B DOIT?).
  3. The 12 pads of that bank get the sound, tuned to the scale: pad 1 = root, then up the scale. CANCEL stops.

Pad Mute / Solo

Mute or solo single pads while the beat plays.

PAD MUTE BANK A M.S.-....... MUT

Hit a pad to mute it (or solo it in SOL mode). DO IT brings all pads back. Mutes stay on when you leave the page. Shortcut: SHIFT+TRACK MUTE. Note: saving the program while pads are muted saves them at level 0.

How to use
  1. SHIFT+TRACK MUTE (or MODE → OTHER → Pad Mute).
  2. The bottom line shows the 12 pads of the bank: M muted, S solo, - silenced by a solo, . playing. BANK A–D changes bank.
  3. Hit a pad to mute or unmute it. Turn MUT to SOL to solo pads instead.
  4. DO IT brings all pads back. Mutes stay on when you leave the page: unmute before saving the program.

Seq Tools

Double or halve the current sequence in one press.

SEQ TOOLS 8 BAR DOUBLE x2

DOUBLE makes the sequence twice as long and copies its bars after the end (all tracks): 2 → 4 → 8 bars. HALF keeps the first half.

How to use
  1. MODE → OTHER → Seq Tools. The top line shows the bars of the current sequence.
  2. Pick DOUBLE x2 or HALF /2 with the wheel, press DO IT.

USB MIDI

The MPC's USB port becomes a MIDI interface: plug it into your computer, no drivers.

Pads and sequences go to your DAW as MPC500 MIDI, like the 5-pin MIDI OUT. Turn it on in its page. With MIDI Screen it also carries the screen.

How to use
  1. MODE → OTHER → USB MIDI, turn it ON with the wheel. Connect the MPC's USB port to your computer.
  2. Your computer sees a MIDI device called MPC500 MIDI (no driver). Pick it as input in your DAW.
  3. It sends what the 5-pin MIDI OUT sends: if nothing arrives, check the MPC's own MIDI settings (track MIDI channel, Soft thru).
  4. Turn it OFF to give the USB port back to the MPC.

MIDI Screen

The MPC's display, waveform, slices, pads and mixer on a big screen, in your browser.

Load MIDISCR.WAV (and USBMIDI.WAV for the USB cable), turn them on, then open the screen page in Chrome and press Connect (allow MIDI). Works over USB or a MIDI interface.

How to use
  1. Load MIDISCR.WAV (it starts ON; its page is MODE → OTHER → MIDI Screen). For the USB cable also load USBMIDI.WAV and turn it ON.
  2. Connect the MPC to the computer: USB cable, or the MPC's MIDI ports to a MIDI interface.
  3. Open the screen page in Chrome, press Connect and allow MIDI (SysEx).
  4. You see the MPC's display, the waveform of the current sample with its slices, the pads and the mixer.

HUB

The add-on manager. Load it first: it installs every add-on you load after it (up to 16 at the same time) and adds them to the OTHER menu.

At power on it shows the OPEN 500 splash. If a tool ever breaks, it tells you at the next start (MEM ERR / CRASH: with the tool name).

How to use
  1. LOAD HUB.WAV first, then the tools (or put them all in the autoload folder). The first time after power on you see the OPEN 500 splash.
  2. The tools appear in MODE → OTHER, after Version.
  3. A tool doesn't show up? SHIFT+ERASE reloads the add-on manager.

Install

Once. Takes about five minutes. You need a CompactFlash card and a computer.

  1. Get the official OS 1.31

    Download the MPC500 OS 1.31 from Akai Professional and unzip it: you get mpc500.bin.

    Download OS 1.31 from Akai (288 KB)

  2. Add Open500 to it

    Drop mpc500.bin here. It is patched in your browser: nothing is uploaded.

    Drop mpc500.bin here or click to choose it

      We never distribute Akai's firmware: this page only adds the Open500 loader (1.3 KB of our own code) to the file you provide, checks that it is the untouched OS 1.31, and recomputes the checksum. The result is identical, byte for byte, to the firmware we tested.

    • Flash it

      Copy MPC500.bin to the root of the CompactFlash card and insert it in the MPC500. Hold SHIFT while you switch the MPC on, then press REC when the screen asks for it. Wait for the update to finish.

    • Load the add-ons

      Copy HUB.WAV and the add-ons you want (CHOPSHOP.WAV, MASTERFX.WAV, GRID.WAV, SAMPLEFX.WAV, PADTOOLS.WAV, SCALEPAD.WAV, PADMUTE.WAV, SEQTOOLS.WAV, INTHRU.WAV, USBMIDI.WAV, MIDISCR.WAV) to the card. On the MPC, LOAD HUB.WAV first (the first time after power on you see the OPEN 500 splash), then the add-ons (LOADED <name>).

      Open them from MODE → OTHER: the add-ons are listed after Version. Select one, press DO IT. CANCEL goes back.

    Add-ons live in memory like samples. To have them ready at every power on, put HUB.WAV and the add-ons in the MPC's autoload folder on the card: they load by themselves, in any order. Otherwise load them again after switching the MPC off.

    Safety & going back

    • Open500 does not touch the bootloader. If anything goes wrong, the MPC can always be updated again.
    • To remove it: flash the original mpc500.bin (OS 1.31) the same way (SHIFT at power on, then REC).
    • Open500 reserves the last 896 KB of sample memory: 384 KB for the add-ons, 512 KB for the Master FX delay / reverb buffers.
    • Messages on the second line: NO FREE SLOT (no room for more add-ons), BAD (damaged WAV), LOW MEM: ADDONS! (sample memory almost full), MEM ERR <name> (that add-on's memory was overwritten: it is stopped), CRASH:<name> (the MPC stopped inside that add-on last time). SHIFT+ERASE reloads the add-on manager.
    • After switching the MPC off, wait about 10 seconds before switching it on again: a fast power cycle can leave the memory in a bad state and show a CPU ADDR error.

    Make your own tool (with Claude)

    You don't need to be a programmer. The Open500 repository includes a skill for Claude Code: it knows the add-on SDK, the official tools and the MPC500's memory, so you can describe the tool you want in plain words and Claude builds it, tests it in an emulator of the MPC and gives you the WAV.

    1. Get Claude Code

      Install Claude Code (terminal or desktop app) and sign in with your Claude account. You also need Python 3 on your computer (macOS already has it).

    2. Download the Open500 repository

      On GitHub: Code → Download ZIP (and unzip it), or git clone https://github.com/M45K/open500.git.

    3. Add your firmware

      Make your MPC500.bin with the installer above (the same file you flash) and put a copy in the open500 folder. The emulator runs it to test your tool. It stays on your computer: never share it.

    4. Open the folder in Claude Code and describe your tool

      Open the open500 folder in Claude Code: the open500-addon skill loads by itself. Then just say what you want, for example:

      "Make a page that tunes the 12 pads of bank B to a minor chord."

      Claude first shows you how the page will look on the 16×2 display and what each button does, then writes the tool, runs its emulator test and fixes it until it passes.

    5. Try it on the MPC

      Copy the new WAV to the card next to HUB.WAV and LOAD it like the other add-ons. Claude gives you a short checklist of what to press and what you should see and hear.

    Tested in the emulator is not the same as tested on the MPC: try new tools with your work saved. If something goes wrong, SHIFT+ERASE reloads the add-on manager, switching off clears all add-ons, and the original OS can always be flashed back. Made something nice? Submit it (or ask Claude to "prepare the Open500 submission"): once approved it appears in Community tools. To use the skill outside the repository, copy .claude/skills/open500-addon to ~/.claude/skills/. Prefer to write the code yourself? Start from sdk/README.md.

    FAQ

    Does it work on the MPC1000 / MPC2500?

    Not yet. Open500 is built on a detailed study of the MPC500 OS 1.31; other models run different software.

    Does it change how my MPC500 works?

    No. Without add-ons loaded, the OS behaves like the original 1.31 — the only difference is 896 KB less sample memory, reserved for add-ons and effects. Your programs, samples and sequences are not affected.

    Why do I have to provide the OS file myself?

    Because the operating system belongs to Akai. Open500 only publishes its own code, and your browser combines the two on your computer.

    Can I write my own add-on?

    Yes, that is the idea. The add-on SDK is in the repository: Python, an emulator to test your add-on before trying it on the MPC, and the source of every official add-on. There is also a skill for Claude Code that knows the SDK and helps you build a tool even if you don't code: see Make your own tool.

    Which browsers work with the screen page?

    Chrome (and other Chromium browsers with Web MIDI) on Mac, PC and Android. It is a beta: tell us in an issue how it goes on your setup.

    I have an idea for an add-on

    Great — open an issue in the repository and describe it.

    How can I support the project?

    Open500 is free. If it is useful to you, a donation helps keep it going: paypal.me/SupportOpen500.
